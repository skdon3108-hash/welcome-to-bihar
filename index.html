About Bihar

Bihar is a state in eastern India. It is one of the oldest historical regions of India and has a rich culture and heritage. The capital of Bihar is Patna.

Bihar is famous for its ancient universities, religious places, festivals, traditional food and Madhubani paintings.

Famous Places in Bihar

1. Bodh Gaya

Bodh Gaya is a famous Buddhist pilgrimage site where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment.



2. Nalanda

Nalanda was an ancient centre of learning and is famous for its historical university.

3. Rajgir

Rajgir is an ancient historical city known for its religious and archaeological importance.

4. Vaishali

Vaishali is an important ancient city associated with Buddhism and Jainism.

5. Patna

Patna is the capital city of Bihar and an important historical and cultural centre.

Historical Importance

Bihar has played an important role in Indian history. The region was home to powerful ancient empires and important centres of education.

  • Magadha Empire
  • Maurya Empire
  • Gupta Empire
  • Ancient Nalanda University
  • Important Buddhist and Jain sites
